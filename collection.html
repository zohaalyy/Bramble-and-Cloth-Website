About these drawings

The pictures above are technical line drawings of each garment rather than photographs. They show the seams, the closures and the actual shape of a piece, which a styled photograph often hides. If you would like to see a garment on a body before you travel, ring us and we will describe the fit honestly, including who it does not suit.

What happens if it does not fit

  1. Try it in the shop

    Nothing is bought unseen. If you have booked a fitting the room is yours for an hour.

  2. We pin it there and then

    Our seamstress is in on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. If she is not in, we pin it and she checks the pinning before cutting.

  3. Collect it in about ten days

    Two alterations are included in the price. We will tell you before you buy if a piece cannot be altered the way you want.
