Access

  • Level entrance directly from the pavement, no step
  • Doorway 90 cm wide, shop floor clear to 1.2 metres throughout
  • Fitting room 1.6 metres square with a bench and a grab rail
  • Assistance dogs very welcome, water bowl behind the counter
  • No accessible toilet on the premises. Nearest is in the village car park

If anything here would stop you visiting, tell us on the form below or when you book a fitting. We would rather change how we do something than lose the customer.
