    Why it gives you the larger size

    If your three measurements land on different sizes, the finder recommends the largest of them. Cloth can be taken in during an alteration but it cannot be conjured out of nothing, and taking in a waist is a twenty minute job. This is the same rule our fitters use in the shop, and both alterations are included in the price.

    How to measure on your own

    1. Stand normally

      Do not hold your breath or brace. Measure the body you have on an ordinary day, because that is the body the garment has to fit.

    2. Keep the tape level

      A mirror helps more than a second person. The tape should sit flat all the way round, not slope down at the back.

    3. Snug, not tight

      You should be able to slip one finger under the tape. Pulling it tight is the single most common reason a size comes out too small.

    Between two sizes?

    Take the larger one. The waist is the easiest seam on any of our garments to bring in, and it is included in the price.

    Outside the chart?
