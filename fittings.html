Fittings

An hour in the room, on your own terms

Free, no obligation to buy anything, and the fitting room is yours for the whole hour.

What a fitting actually is

You tell us roughly what you are looking for and what size you think you are. We pull those pieces in two or three sizes each and have them hanging in the room before you arrive, so you are not walking the shop floor collecting hangers.

Someone stays nearby to fetch other sizes, but nobody comes in unless you ask them to. If you want a second opinion you can have one, and if you would rather be left alone you say so on the form and that is what happens.

Thursday evenings between 17:30 and 19:00 are appointment only, so the shop is empty apart from other people who have booked.

Before you come

  • Use the size finder so we pull the right sizes
  • Bring the shoes you would wear with it, if hem length matters
  • Allow a full hour, and a little longer on a Saturday
  • Bring whoever you like, or come on your own

Accessibility

The shop is on the ground floor with a level entrance from Alcester Road. The fitting room is 1.6 metres square with a bench and a grab rail, and the door opens outwards.

There is no accessible toilet on the premises. The nearest is in Moseley Village car park, about three minutes away.

We can lower the lighting, turn the music off, and hold the shop closed to other customers for a fitting. Say so on the form and we will do it without being asked twice.
