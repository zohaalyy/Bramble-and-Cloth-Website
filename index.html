Why we do it this way

Most occasionwear is designed for a UK 12 and graded outwards until it stops fitting anyone. We start from three fit blocks rather than one, so a size 24 is drafted as a size 24 instead of being a stretched size 12.

That is also why alterations are included. A garment that fits at the shoulder and gapes at the waist is not a bad body, it is an unfinished garment. Our seamstress is in the shop three days a week and the first two alterations on anything you buy from us are free.

We are a shop of four people above a bakery on Alcester Road. There is no online checkout, because we would rather you tried the thing on. What this site does is tell you what we have, what size you are likely to need, and when you can come in.

If you have been sized out of shops before

Plenty of our customers arrive expecting to be told nothing fits. Everything on the rail is in every size we list, on the rail, in the shop, today. You do not need to order it in and you do not need to ask whether it comes in your size. It does.

If your measurements sit outside UK 8 to 28, we make to measure from the same fabrics. Ring us on 0121 449 0224 and we will talk it through.
